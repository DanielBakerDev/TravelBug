How it works

Four steps and you're on the plane

  1. 1

    Pick a trip

    Read the day-by-day and see if the pace suits you. Not sure? Message me — I'll tell you honestly whether it's your kind of thing.

  2. 2

    Hold your spot

    A deposit reserves your place. The balance isn't due until eight weeks before you go.

  3. 3

    I do the boring bits

    Accommodation, permits, internal transport, guides, the restaurant that needs booking three months out. You book your own flights, and I'll tell you which ones.

  4. 4

    We go

    Twelve of us, one guide, and a group chat that stays annoyingly active long after everyone's home.

About

Hi — I'm Amanda

I started organising trips because I kept being the person in the group chat with the spreadsheet. Eventually somebody suggested I charge for it.

I run four or five departures a year and I'm on every one of them. That's the limit of what I can do properly, and I'd rather run a handful of good trips than a calendar full of average ones.

Most people who come have travelled plenty — they've just had enough of doing the logistics themselves.
