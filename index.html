How it works

Four steps, and I take it from there

  1. 01

    A conversation

    Thirty minutes, no cost. Where you're thinking, who's coming, what "a good trip" means to you. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so.

  2. 02

    A first draft

    I come back with a route, a budget range, and two or three shapes the trip could take — so you're choosing between good options, not starting from scratch.

  3. 03

    We refine it

    You tell me what to change. We go back and forth until the plan feels like yours, then I book and confirm everything.

  4. 04

    You go

    You get a clean day-by-day guide before you leave, and my number while you're away — for the flight that moves or the restaurant that cancels.

Questions

Before you ask

What does it cost?

Planning fees depend on the length and complexity of the trip, and I'll quote you a flat figure after our first conversation — before you commit to anything.

Is it more expensive than booking myself?

Usually not. I have partner rates and perks at many properties that aren't available publicly, which often offsets a good portion of the fee.

How far ahead should I book?

Four to six months is comfortable for most trips. Peak season, big groups, and honeymoons want longer. If it's sooner than that, ask anyway.

What if something goes wrong while I'm away?

You call me. Rebookings, cancellations, a hotel that lost your reservation — that's included, and it's the part people say they value most.
