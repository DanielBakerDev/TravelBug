How it works

Four steps and you're on the plane

Some of our adventures take us off the tourist track and into the complete unknown. We set up a video call to give you more fine details about the program, and make sure you are a match for the group you will be paired with. Interested? Fill in this form to set up your call.

  1. 1

    Choose your adventure.

    Read the day-by-day and see if the tour suits you, then fill in this form to nab your video call with a team member.

  2. 2

    Keep your spot!

    A 500 euro deposit reserves your spot. The full balance is due three months before our tour begins.

  3. 3

    I do the boring stuff.

    I book accommodation, internal transport, park permits, local flights & restaurants. I hire tour guides, drivers, local park guides, a photographer, and climbing guides to ensure you have a great adventure.

  4. 4

    We adventure!

    A small yet mighty group, one guide, and a group chat that stays annoyingly active long after everyone's home. Lifelong memories, and a whole load of photos and videos to look back at when you're home.

About

Hey there, I'm Amanda

On a major cross-continent trip 10 years ago, I met my travel soulmates. Each year since the Mongol Rally in 2016, I have been organizing super adventurous trips for our friends & friends of friends. With our group forever expanding, and more bucket list destinations to tick off, we decided to put together a lil company and invite others along to have the time of their lives.

We run 4–6 departures a year, led by a fantastic tour leader that organizes everything for you. Without worries, you get to sit back, relax, get involved in all group activities, & enjoy all the sights. Each day is an adventure with like-minded explorers who are keen for daily adventure.

Our group members are mostly well-travelled, but do not have the time to worry about logistics.
