Vietnam 2 spots left Easy–moderate

North to South

12 days · 8–19 October 2026 · from £2,480

The idea

Vietnam is long and most people try to do all of it in a fortnight. We don't. This route takes the north slowly — two days walking between villages in the Sa Pa valley, a night on the water in Ha Long Bay — then flies south so the last third is warm, flat and unhurried.

There's one early start for the boat and one overnight train. Everything else is civilised.

Day by day

  1. 1

    Arrive Hanoi

    Land whenever suits — I'll arrange the airport transfer. We eat together in the Old Quarter in the evening and I run through the plan.

  2. 2

    Hanoi on foot

    A morning walking the Old Quarter with a guide who grew up in it, the afternoon free. Overnight train north.

  3. 3

    Into the Sa Pa valley

    Arrive Lao Cai at dawn and transfer up. An easy afternoon walk to shake off the train and get used to the altitude.

  4. 4

    Sa Pa — Ta Van and Giang Ta Chai

    The big walking day. Five hours between villages on terraced paths, lunch with a family in Ta Van, homestay overnight.

  5. 5

    Sa Pa — second day out

    A shorter loop through bamboo forest and back down to the valley floor. Afternoon free, then the night train back.

  6. 6

    Ha Long Bay

    Straight from Hanoi to the coast and onto the boat. Swimming, kayaking round the karsts, and dinner on deck.

  7. 7

    Bay morning, then south

    Sunrise on the water and a cave before breakfast, back to shore by lunch, evening flight to Da Nang and on to Hoi An.

  8. 8

    Hoi An

    The old town in the morning, a cooking class in the afternoon if you fancy it, and the lanterns after dark.

  9. 9

    Hoi An — your day

    Nothing scheduled. Most people cycle to the beach, some go to My Son, one person always ends up at the tailor twice.

  10. 10

    Ho Chi Minh City

    Morning flight south. Afternoon in District 1, dinner somewhere loud.

  11. 11

    Mekong Delta day trip

    Out to the water, into a smaller boat, and back in time for a last dinner together.

  12. 12

    Home

    Transfers to the airport for any flight time.
