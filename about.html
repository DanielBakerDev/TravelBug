Hi — I'm Amanda

I run four or five trips a year, and I'm on all of them.

How this started

I was always the one in the group chat with the spreadsheet — the flights tab, the colour-coded accommodation, the running total nobody asked for. After the fourth or fifth trip somebody pointed out I could charge for this.

So I did, carefully. The first departure was nine friends-of-friends to Georgia in 2023 and I made most of the mistakes there, which was the cheapest place to make them.

Why twelve people

Twelve is the largest group that still fits round one table at dinner and into one minibus. Past that you need a second vehicle, a second guide, and a schedule that runs to the slowest person — and it stops feeling like a group of people who know each other.

It also means I can say yes to places that don't work at scale: the guesthouse with six rooms, the family that cooks for you, the permit that's capped.

What the trips are actually like

Busy but not frantic. There's usually a hard day in the middle and a slow one after it. I build in unscheduled afternoons because the best bits normally happen in them.

People come solo more often than not, and I don't charge a single supplement for a shared room. Nobody has ever regretted booking on their own.

I'm not a guide in the qualified-mountain-leader sense — for anything technical I hire local people who are, and pay them properly. What I do is everything else.

What you can expect

You'll get on with this if…

  • You've travelled a fair bit and want the logistics gone
  • You're happy eating with eleven other people most nights
  • You'd rather walk into a place than be driven past it
  • You're coming alone and that's fine by you

Probably not for you if…

  • You want a fixed schedule with no unplanned time
  • You'd prefer a private trip just for your group
  • Five-star everything is the point of the holiday
  • You need to be back at the hotel by six
